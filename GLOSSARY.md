# SPK

SPK is a self-hosted chat and voice app. This glossary records the language shared by its web and desktop clients.

## Language

### People and access

**SPK Instance**

One self-hosted installation of SPK with its own accounts and channels.

**SPK User**

A person with an account on an SPK instance.

**Profile**

The SPK identity shown to other users, including the user’s nickname and avatar.

**User Presence**

A user’s availability as shown across the instance. Choosing **Invisible** shows the user as offline in user presence while their voice participation can still be visible.

**Invite Link**

A single-use link for creating an account on an SPK instance.

**Admin**

An SPK user with the Admin role, which grants access to instance administration and moderation.

**User Role**

The standard permission level, shown as **User** in SPK. It grants access to chat and voice.

**Ban**

A moderation action that removes an account’s access to the instance while keeping its existing messages.

### Channels and chat

**Channel**

A named, shared destination in an SPK instance for text chat or live audio and video.

_Avoid using “channel” for a DM conversation._

**Text Channel**

A channel with a shared message history that signed-in users on the instance can access.

**Direct Message (DM) Conversation**

A private conversation with a shared message history between two SPK users.

**Message**

An individual post in a text channel or DM conversation.

**Draft**

An unsent message being composed for a specific text channel or DM conversation.

**Message Composer**

The chat area where a user prepares and sends a message, including the draft editor and its controls.

**Attachment**

A file included with a message.

**Spoiler**

Text or an attachment hidden until a reader chooses to reveal it.

**Reply**

A message that points to an earlier message in the same text channel or DM conversation.

_Avoid using “thread” for a reply._

**Quote**

An excerpt from another message included as text in a message.

**Mention**

A direct reference to an SPK user by their nickname in a message.

**Reaction**

An emoji a user adds to a message as a response.

**Pin**

A mark that keeps a message accessible from the pinned messages list in its text channel or DM conversation.

**Favorite**

A GIF or radio station a user saves for their own reuse.

**Unread**

The state of another user’s message sent after your last read point in a text channel or DM conversation.

**Ping**

A message that mentions a user or replies to one of their messages.

**Mute Notifications**

A per-user setting that silences message alerts for a text channel or DM conversation. Unread messages still accumulate.

### Voice and video

**Voice Channel**

A named channel that users can join to talk, listen, and share live video.

_Avoid using “room” or “voice room” for a voice channel._

**Voice Participant**

An SPK user who has joined a voice channel.

**Voice Session**

A user’s voice participation through a specific SPK client. Each user has one active voice session at most, so joining from another client replaces the previous session.

**Voice Presence**

SPK’s shared view of who has joined each voice channel and each voice participant’s mute, deafen, and speaking states.

**Listen Only**

A voice connection that can play incoming audio while the user’s microphone is unavailable.

**Mute**

The voice control that silences the user’s microphone while keeping incoming audio available.

**Deafen**

The voice control that silences both the user’s microphone and incoming audio.

**Speaking**

Detected voice activity from a participant’s microphone.

**Mute for Me**

A personal playback control that silences another participant’s microphone and screen-share audio for the listener.

**Camera**

A voice participant’s live camera video.

**Screen Share**

A voice participant’s live view of a selected screen, window, or tab, optionally with its audio.

**Watch**

A viewer’s choice to receive the camera video, screen share, and screen audio a participant is sharing.

**Channel Radio**

A radio station selected for a voice channel. The station is shared, and each listener controls their own playback.

**Soundboard**

A collection of short audio clips that voice participants can play for the channel.

**Voice Sounds**

Automatic audio cues for voice events such as joining or leaving a channel, muting, or deafening.

### Audio devices

**Preferred Device**

The specific microphone or speakers a user has saved as their choice for voice.

**Active Device**

The microphone or speakers currently assigned to voice capture or playback.

**System Default**

The operating system’s current choice of microphone or speakers. SPK can follow it by preference or use it while a preferred device is unavailable.

**Voice Output**

The audio destination for sound from other voice participants’ microphones.

**Media Output**

The audio destination for screen-share audio and other media playback, including radio, soundboard clips, and media attachments.
