dfn3_rs implements DeepFilterNet3 inference.

DSP, streaming conventions, and model parameters are derived from DeepFilterNet
by Hendrik Schroeter and contributors:
https://github.com/Rikorose/DeepFilterNet
Upstream revision: 7b14b895fe7fc42cab72b4a4c1e49c982bd6404b
Upstream model archive: models/DeepFilterNet3_onnx.tar.gz

weights/dfn3.bin contains reordered and padded matrices and folded convolution
weights. Encoder matrices are FP32; decoder matrices are IEEE binary16. Biases
and convolution taps remain FP32. SHA256:
6f6148765e2ded639370d7ee6b4e6a39989c7900986461ac0cd826459904cd4e
